## What is this?

BADI is a business analytics prototype created during Bangkit 2022. It helps small-business owners inspect income trends and explore forecasts through a shared application.

## The problem and why it matters

Transaction records become useful when someone can see what changed and what might happen next. The team aimed to make those patterns easier to access without requiring a business owner to work directly with a model.

## My role

I led the machine learning work and collaborated with Android and Cloud Computing teammates. My work connected data preparation and predictive components to the application.

## Approach

The prototype combined income analysis with forecasting. The existing project story describes 7–30 day predictions, anomaly exploration, seasonal patterns, and comparisons. These are prototype capabilities described by the team, not validated claims of business impact.

## Architecture and implementation

Python handled the analytical work. Flask exposed the components to the application, with scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, and TensorFlow included in the original stack.

<Technical title="The boundary between a model and a usable application">
  The important integration problem was agreeing on inputs and outputs across
  machine learning, Android, and cloud work. A useful response needs predictable
  structure and understandable meaning, not just a numerical prediction.

## Data and technical decisions

The original portfolio does not supply a reproducible benchmark dataset, model evaluation table, or production monitoring record. Those details should be sourced from the team repository before adding model-performance claims.

## Outcome

The team completed the prototype and presented it at Bangkit graduation. The existing source describes functional predictions; there are no verified adoption, revenue, or external production-performance figures.

## Limitations

A completed prototype is not evidence of forecasting accuracy for new businesses. Data quality, time horizon, and changing business conditions all require evaluation before operational use.

## What I learned

Team interfaces matter as much as individual model experiments. The analytical work has to fit the way someone enters data, receives a result, and understands uncertainty.
