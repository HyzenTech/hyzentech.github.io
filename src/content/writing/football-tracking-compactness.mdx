<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="positions">

Start with **one observed passage**, not a team-wide judgement: 298.88–310.12 seconds in period one of Metrica Sample Game 1.

Each marker is a real Home outfield position. The ball and anonymized player identifiers stay traceable to the pinned source.

<Technical title="Coordinates and eligibility">

A 105 × 68 m pitch converts normalized x and flipped y coordinates into metres. Only the Home half is shown for legibility. Player11 is excluded as goalkeeper by minimum distance of median x to the defended goal. There are 257 valid analysis frames after ball-coordinate exclusions.

</Technical>

</Step>

<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="shape">

Connect the outside players and mark the mean position. The team now has a visible **boundary and centre**.

Width is the range of y positions; length is the range of x positions. The convex hull shows spatial context, while these two axis ranges measure spread.

<Technical title="Definitions">

Centroid is the arithmetic mean of valid outfield positions. Width=max(y)-min(y), length=max(x)-min(x). The initial centroid is (24.311, 44.604) m, width 30.861 m, length 25.481 m. Hull area is not used as the article’s compactness metric.

</Technical>

</Step>

<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="disruption">

At **303.08 seconds**, the distance between the Home centroid and the ball reaches **22.007 metres**.

The same player markers move to the next observed snapshot. The measurement adds context to shape; it does not explain why the team moved.

<Technical title="Observed snapshots, not reconstructed motion">

The animation connects real sampled positions for explanation. It is not a replay of every tracking frame or a measured interpolated trajectory. Distance is Euclidean between the outfield centroid and ball.

</Technical>

</Step>

<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="compactness">

By 310.12 seconds, Home width falls from **30.861 to 24.900 metres**. Length changes from 25.481 to 24.180 metres.

The centroid moves **5.073 metres** overall, including 3.876 metres toward the defended goal. A smaller spread describes the shape; it does not prove successful defending.

<Technical title="Endpoint and extrema">

Final centroid=(20.435, 41.330) m; centroid-to-ball distance=6.594 m versus 4.853 initially. Width peaks at 33.802 m at 307.08; length reaches 21.660 m at 303.36. The source summary and derived metric CSV supply these values.

</Technical>

</Step>

<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="gap">

Between the observed frames at **307.92 and 308.96 seconds**, ball positions are missing. The trace breaks; it does not draw an invented path across the gap.

The event record identifies **Away recovery at 308.96**, correcting the legacy article’s Home-regain wording. An event label and a tracked position are different kinds of evidence.

<Technical title="Source correction and missing data">

The synchronized source summary identifies the 308.96 event as Away / RECOVERY / Player21. Missing-ball frames are excluded from the analysis, leaving 257 valid frames. The 1.04-second interval is shown even though a chart could conceal it.

</Technical>

</Step>

<Step story="football-tracking-compactness" id="interpretation">

The passage ends with an **Away off-target shot** at 310.12 seconds. Keep that event timeline beside the spatial measurements.

This is a descriptive study of one sequence. It cannot establish causation, coaching intent, or a general defensive-quality rating.
